Zero connectors blocked — DLP policy is effectively decorative

With 1,534 connectors all sitting in General, there is no separation between business-sensitive connectors (SharePoint, Teams, Dataverse, Exchange) and consumer/personal connectors (Twitter/X, Dropbox, Gmail, personal OneDrive). A maker can create a flow that reads from SharePoint and posts to any social platform — with no DLP barrier.

Remediation: Move high-risk consumer connectors to Blocked. Move sensitive business connectors to Business/Confidential. Separate them from General to enforce the data boundary.

Default classification is General — new connectors auto-permissive

When Microsoft releases new connectors, they are automatically assigned the General bucket (the permissive default). This means new AI, data, or 3rd-party connectors enter the tenant fully usable without any admin review.

Set defaultConnectorsClassification to Blocked in the policy so new connectors require explicit admin approval before use.

Policy scope is ExceptEnvironments — verify excluded envs are not high-risk

The ExceptEnvironments scope means certain environments are explicitly excluded from this policy. If those excluded environments are production or contain business data, they may operate without any DLP controls.

pac admin dlp-policy show --policy-name "fabd6dbe-63d5-4f16-a4dc-60c0a732735c" — review the excluded environment list
